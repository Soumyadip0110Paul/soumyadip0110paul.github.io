about

I design quantum and hybrid quantum-classical algorithms for the hard optimization problems inside next-generation wireless networks.

I’m a doctoral researcher at the Centre for Applied Research in Electronics, IIT Delhi, advised by Prof. Neel Kanth Kundu. Recent work includes a warm-start QAOA for QAM MIMO detection, run on IBM quantum hardware, and a quantum algorithm for phase optimization in 1-bit RIS-assisted MIMO. Before that I worked on post-quantum cryptography and quantum-resistant blockchains.
