The Future of Connectivity.

I am a doctoral researcher at the Centre for Applied Research in Electronics (CARE), IIT Delhi, working with Prof. Neel Kanth Kundu. My research explores quantum machine learning and hybrid quantum-classical optimization for next-generation (6G and beyond) wireless networks.

Recent work includes a warm-start linear-ramp QAOA (WSLR-QAOA) for QAM MIMO data detection, formulated as a higher-order unconstrained binary optimization (HUBO) problem and validated on IBM quantum hardware, and an efficient quantum algorithm for phase optimization of 1-bit RIS-assisted MIMO systems. My earlier work spans post-quantum cryptography, quantum-resistant blockchain and hybrid QAOA scheduling. 🌐⚡
