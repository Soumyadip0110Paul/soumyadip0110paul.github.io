About

I am a doctoral researcher at CARE, IIT Delhi, working with Prof. Neel Kanth Kundu on quantum machine learning and quantum optimization for 6G and beyond wireless networks.

My current work develops hybrid quantum-classical algorithms for physical-layer problems: a warm-start linear-ramp QAOA for QAM MIMO data detection, validated on IBM quantum hardware, and an efficient quantum algorithm for phase optimization in 1-bit RIS-assisted MIMO systems. During my M.Tech I worked on post-quantum cryptography and quantum-resistant blockchains. Away from research, I trek in the Himalayas.
